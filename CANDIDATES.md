## Pick rationale

**Unsubscribed** wins the pairwise game against every other candidate above: it is
the only one that is simultaneously (a) unmistakably a verdict on an ex,
(b) a joke rather than an insult, (c) surprising, (d) one clean word, and
(e) universally recognised. The subscription metaphor does the most work with
the fewest letters, and the flatness of the word *is* the punchline.

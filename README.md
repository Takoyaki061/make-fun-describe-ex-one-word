Entry for the [Describe Your Ex in One Word](https://make.fun/bounty/u-puan-describe-your-ex-in-one-word-9fc6ebe7) bounty on make.fun.

`main.py` writes exactly one word to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Unsubscribed

You brace for an insult and get a piece of admin instead — the relationship framed as a subscription you cancelled. No fight, no drama, just the quiet opt-out: the ex was a service that stopped being worth the fee. The joke lands at a glance because everyone has unsubscribed from something, and the metaphor turns a breakup into the most boring, bureaucratic breakup possible — which is exactly what makes it sting.

See [CANDIDATES.md](CANDIDATES.md) for the 10 candidates considered and why this word was picked.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Stdlib only, deterministic.
